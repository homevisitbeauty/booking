Questions

Before you book

Can I book on the same day?

Yes, same-day bookings are welcome. WhatsApp us directly for the fastest response and we'll confirm your slot right away.

Do you serve hotel guests?

Absolutely. Most of our clients are hotel guests. Simply give us your hotel name and room number and we'll handle the rest.

What wax types do you offer?

We carry three options: RICA (premium Italian liposoluble), Aloevera (gentle, soothing), and Honey (natural, traditional). Let us know your preference when booking.

Is the service private and discreet?

Completely. Your appointment is conducted entirely in your own private space. Our beautician arrives professionally and discreetly.

What areas of Bangkok do you cover?

All districts — from Sukhumvit to Silom, Siam to Bangna. If you're in Bangkok, we can reach you. Just ask.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before your appointment at no charge. We understand plans change, especially when travelling.

Is payment in Thai Baht?

Yes, payment is in Thai Baht (THB). We accept cash upon completion of the service. Enquire about rates via WhatsApp.

Do you offer morning or evening appointments?

Yes, we offer flexible scheduling including early mornings, evenings, and weekends. Just mention your preferred time when you book.

Ready to book your session?

Same-day slots available across Bangkok. Takes 60 seconds to book via WhatsApp.
